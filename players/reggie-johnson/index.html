About Reggie

Men's First Team · #12

Reggie Johnson has helped set the standard for Firelands United's attack. The Lorain native led the club in scoring in both 2025 and 2026 and is its all-time leading goal scorer with 19 goals. His 10 goals across all competitions in 2026 set the club's single-season scoring record, and he shares the all-time appearance lead with Coen Steinmetz and Jonah Main. A 2024 graduate of Lorain High School, Reggie earned Player of the Year honors during his senior season. He now plays college soccer at Hiram College, where he is a junior studying biology.
