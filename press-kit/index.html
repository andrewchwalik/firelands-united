01 / The Club

Description

Firelands United Football Club is a community soccer club representing the Firelands region of Northern Ohio. Founded in 2023, the club provides competitive opportunities for local players and brings supporters together through the game.

The men's first team began competing in 2025, followed by the women's first team in 2026. Both teams compete in the Northern Ohio Soccer League (NOSL) and play home matches at Strobel Field in Sandusky, Ohio.

02 / Our Purpose

Mission

Develop players. Build community. Grow the sport.

Firelands United creates opportunities for local players to keep developing and competing while representing their region. Through soccer, we bring people together, build pride in our community, and help the game grow across the Firelands.
