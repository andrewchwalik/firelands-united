Welcome to the Lands!

Your agreement

Welcome to the Lands! We're excited to offer you a spot on our Firelands United roster.

At Firelands United, we strive to provide an experience that rivals the standards of top semi-professional and professional clubs across the country. Our mission is to develop players, build community, and grow the sport.

Read through the following contract. If you agree to everything, e-sign at the bottom. By completing and submitting this form, you agree to all lease terms and understand that your typed name constitutes your legally binding electronic signature under Ohio law (UETA and E-Sign Act). Once you click that "submit" button, you'll officially be a member of Firelands United Football Club!

1.0 Parties

1.1 This CONTRACT is between the signee hereinafter referred to as “PLAYER” and Firelands United Football Club (“FUFC”) (“the Club”).

1.2 Thank you for your willingness to represent Firelands United Football Club (FUFC) on the field and in public. The purpose of this contract is to provide you with information about different club policies and to obtain your agreement to these policies while outlining your responsibilities as a PLAYER for FUFC and FUFC’s responsibilities to you.

2.0 Duration of the Agreement

2.1 The term of this CONTRACT is for the FUFC 2027 summer season.

3.0 Player Responsibilities

3.1 You agree to:

  • attend all training sessions, play the games scheduled for the Club’s team during the season, play all exhibition games scheduled by the Club during and prior to the scheduled season to the best of your ability;
  • pay the club fees (around $285: this includes your 2 full uniforms, training shirts, training costs, league fees, etc.)
  • to report, at the time and place fixed by the Club, in good physical condition and keep themselves, throughout the entire season, in good physical condition;
  • report all serious or disabling injuries sustained in scheduled games or practices to the Club as soon as you are reasonably able to report them;
  • to give your best services, as well as loyalty to the Club;
  • to conduct yourself on and off the field according to the highest standard of honesty, morality, fair play, and sportsmanship;
  • comply with all rules and regulations as set forth by FUFC and the league(s) that FUFC is involved with, as well as the policies and procedures of FUFC all of which are subject to revision from time to time;
  • authorize photograph, picture or likeness, and voice to appear in any documentary, promotion (including advertising and with sponsors), television, print, video, social media accounts or radio coverage of FUFC, league or tournament, without compensation;

Time Commitment Details

We're an amateur soccer club with players who are also taking on school and/or work full-time. We recognize this, but we expect players to be able to commit to training & playing in the Northern Ohio area for the duration of our summer season (May to mid-July). Our men's & women's teams play 10-12 games that will usually take place on weekends (with occasional evening/night weekday games), however playing time is not guaranteed. We will have no more than two required trainings per week during evenings/nights. Only submit your information if you can commit to this schedule.

4.0 Health & Safety

4.1 FUFC will, as far as practicable, ensure a safe and healthy environment, the responsibility for personal health, safety, and welfare while undertaking the duties of this agreement rests with the Coaches. The FUFC Coaches will ensure that the duties are conducted taking due care of health, safety, and welfare without putting at risk the health, safety, and welfare of themselves, PLAYERs of FUFC, and members of the public.

5.0 FUFC's Responsibilities

5.1 The Club agrees to:

  • create a unique, exciting, and competitive lower-league soccer experience that champions its players, fans, and community;
  • provide developmental opportunities through scheduled practices and games;
  • provide a dedicated coaching staff;
  • provide jerseys and training gear to new players;
  • advocate for the safety, development, and future of players, with their interest in mind;
  • support players in any manner possible upon request, if such request is deemed necessary and feasible by FUFC. Requests are able to be denied/accepted at FUFC’s discretion.

6.0 Termination

6.1 FUFC reserves the right to terminate this Contract at any time should: (1) PLAYER breach any term or condition of this Agreement or violates FUFC Policy, or federal, state or local law; (2) The Club dissolves; or (3) at FUFC’s sole discretion.

7.0 Severability

7.1 PLAYER and FUFC agree that each provision of this CONTRACT is severable and if any provision is determined by a court of competent jurisdiction or administrative agency to be illegal or invalid, the validity of the remaining provisions shall not be affected and the illegal or invalid provisions shall be deemed not to be a part of this Contract.
