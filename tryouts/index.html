Life at Firelands

Do you have what it takes to play for a minor-league soccer club?

At Firelands United, we strive to provide an experience that rivals the standards of top semi-professional and professional clubs across the country. Our mission is to develop players, build community, and grow the sport.

We're looking for talented players to help us bring home some silverware. Members of our club should also serve as positive role models on the pitch, in the community, and throughout the sport.

What wearing the badge means

  • Regular, high-level training
  • Dedicated coaching and support staff
  • Connections to collegiate and semi-pro teams
  • Every match streamed live
  • Game footage and player content
  • Athletic trainer at every home match
  • Firelands United gear
  • A growing fan base

Age limit

There aren't any age limits to play for Firelands United. All that matters is if you can compete at this level. Players under 18 are required to have the proper waivers signed by a parent or guardian.

Time commitment

We're an amateur soccer club with players also taking on school and/or work full-time. Players are expected to commit to training and playing in Northern Ohio for our summer season, May to mid-July.

Our men's and women's teams play 10–12 games, usually on weekends with occasional evening weekday games. Playing time is not guaranteed. There are no more than two required evening trainings per week. Only submit your information if you can commit to this schedule.

Season player dues (separate from tryouts)

Player dues: TBD ($275 estimate). We try to keep playing affordable. Players receive two Firelands United kits (jersey, shorts, socks) and two training tops.

Dues support league fees, referees, field rental for games and practices, an athletic trainer at each first-team home game, personalized media and live-stream commentary, team bonding experiences, and more.
